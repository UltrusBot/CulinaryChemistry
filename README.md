# Culinary Chemistry

Culinary Chemistry adds multiple blocks and items letting you combine and modify potions and food items with even more planned. 

- **Blender**: Lets you combine food items into a smoothie or multiple potions together.
- **Smoothie**: Multiple food items combined in a blender makes this, retains the status effects given by items like golden apples, and some items give special properties, like chorus fruit, or blaze powder.
- **Status Baster**: Click when you have status effects to remove them and put them in a bottle.
- **Juicer**: Extracts status effects from items to make a potion.
